How Client-Side PDF Decryption Works

DocBrisk processes the binary file directly via WebAssembly instances of `pdf-lib`. When the decryption key is supplied or permissions are stripped, the PDF is reserialized without the encryption dictionary flag. Because this operation completes within the browser engine, confidential documents like salary slips, bank records, and legal briefs never touch a third-party server.
